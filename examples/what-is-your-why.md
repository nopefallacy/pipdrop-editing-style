# Example 1 · "What is your why?" (118 s, personal photos allowed)

**Story:** at a webinar the speaker is asked "what is your why?". The first answer is "to be seen". Digging deeper, it becomes
"to help people who are stuck", and at the bottom it's "to help my mom". The film ends by asking the viewer the same question.
**Turn:** "the only reason why I want to do this is… to help my mom" (82.6 s). **Accent:** gold on "why?" (hook) and "my mom" (iceberg floor).
**Device:** a real iceberg photo visited three times (tip → below the water → the whole berg with the gold floor).
**Music:** Satie, *Gymnopédie No. 1*. It dips almost to silence on "But after further investigation", swells from "to help my mom"
through "That's the whole goal", and fades out under the closing question.
